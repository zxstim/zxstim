## What is EPIC?

**EPIC** (Ethereum for Public Infrastructure and Commons) is the Ethereum Foundation (EF) team that works with government agencies and public institutions. The team sets three principles:

- institutional independence, without being tied to one vendor;
- each citizen's self-determination and consent;
- reasonable cost.

EPIC says it wants to help institutions build their own capacity without depending on the EF, and that it won't work with institutions that go against these principles.

## What EPIC helps with

- **Learning:** briefings and materials for decision-makers, and workshops on designing self-sovereign infrastructure.
- **Exploring:**
  - high-level feedback on the planned technical direction;
  - help identifying needs and jointly exploring non-financial applications;
  - designing community programs, and work on standards.
- **Connecting:** introductions to suitable development teams.

## What EPIC doesn't help with

- Funding just to use or move to Ethereum.
- Ready-made, turnkey products.
- Unlimited technical support.
- Certifying or endorsing any vendor or architecture.
- Influencing the development of the Ethereum protocol.

## How to get in touch

1. Send a short note: what you want to learn about, your goals and your constraints.
2. Talk it over by email or on a call to understand the context, and see whether Ethereum is a good fit.
3. Both sides look at the next step together: material to read, feedback on an early idea, an introduction, or an approach that doesn't use Ethereum.

No formal proposal is needed, and you don't need to have decided in advance to use Ethereum. The contact address is epic@ethereum.org; EPIC says it replies within three business days.

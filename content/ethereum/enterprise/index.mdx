This section is for business owners, banks, financial companies and technology teams considering Ethereum. If you don't yet know what a blockchain is, read the [Basics](../basics/index.mdx) section first.

## Five things worth knowing

1. **Neutral blockspace.** No company or country owns Ethereum, so no competitor can change the rules of the game or block your transactions.
2. **Verify instead of trust.** Partners, auditors and customers can check balances, history and rules for themselves.
3. **Layer 2.** Low fees and high speed, while results are still recorded on Ethereum. Coinbase, Kraken, Sony and Robinhood have all launched their own Layer 2s.
4. **Customization.** A dedicated Layer 2 can choose its fee token, who may take part, where data is stored and who may upgrade it.
5. **Compliance.** Identity, sanctions screening and privacy are handled at the token, Layer 2 and application layers, while the protocol layer stays neutral.

This wiki is an open encyclopedia about Ethereum. The content is open: anyone can read it, copy it and suggest improvements.

The **Basics** section is for everyone. You don't need to know programming or finance. The articles are short and go from everyday examples to the technical terms, and many include animations that show how Ethereum works.

## Where should you start?

**New to Ethereum?** Read the [Getting started](./getting-started/index.mdx) articles. Five short pages explain what Ethereum is, what ETH is for, how wallets work, what gas fees are and how to keep your assets safe.

**Want to know what goes on inside?** The [How Ethereum works](./how-it-works/index.mdx) articles explain blockchains, nodes, consensus, validators, addresses and keys, digital signatures, smart contracts, gas, blocks, Layer 2 and how Ethereum upgrades. Each one comes with an animation.

**Ready to use it?** The [Using Ethereum](./using-ethereum/index.mdx) articles show you how to read a transaction on a block explorer, and explain tokens, stablecoins, NFTs, smart wallets (with two deeper articles on EIP-7702 and ERC-4337) and privacy.

## Three things to remember before using Ethereum

1. **No one can get your money back for you.** Ethereum has no bank and no support hotline. You hold your own assets.
2. **Transactions can't be reversed.** Send to the wrong address and the money is gone. Double-check before you send.
3. **Never share your recovery phrase.** Whoever has it has everything in your wallet.

Ethereum is more than buying and selling crypto on an exchange. It's an open network where you can hold assets, send money anywhere in the world and use apps without permission from any bank or company.

This section is for complete beginners. Read the articles in order:

## What do you need to get started?

Just a device and an internet connection. You don't need a bank account, an ID document or anyone's permission.

The first step is to create a **wallet**: an app that lets you hold assets, send and receive money, and sign in to apps on Ethereum. [Wallets, accounts and recovery phrases](./wallets-and-accounts.mdx) walks you through it in detail.

<Callout type="warn" title="Important">
On Ethereum, there is no customer service team that can get your money back. Read [Staying safe](./staying-safe.mdx) before you move large amounts.
</Callout>

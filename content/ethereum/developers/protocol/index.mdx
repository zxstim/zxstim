The most complete documentation of Ethereum's internals is the [EPF wiki](https://epf.wiki) of the Ethereum Protocol Fellowship program: more than a hundred pages, written for people who want to work on the protocol. This part is adapted from the EPF wiki and ethereum.org's developer docs, condensed to what an app developer needs to know.

Both sources are out of date in places, so every number, date and upgrade status in these pages was checked against the EIPs and specs in November 2026. Each page ends with a **What this means for your app** section, then a **Sources** section that links to the EPF and ethereum.org pages for anyone who wants to go deeper.

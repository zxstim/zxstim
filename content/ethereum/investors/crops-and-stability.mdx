When people are asked what makes Ethereum different from other blockchains, and from everything outside the blockchain world, the answer Vitalik hears most often is: **decentralization and CROPS**.

## What CROPS is

Four properties, according to the Ethereum Foundation's mandate (March 2026):

- **Censorship resistance**: no one can block a valid transaction forever. Vitalik adds **capture resistance**: no group can take control of the protocol.
- **Open source**, and free in the sense of being free to use, modify and share.
- **Privacy**.
- **Security**.

The mandate treats these four properties as a non-negotiable condition for all development on Ethereum. It also sets out the **walkaway test**: the protocol must keep working even if the Ethereum Foundation and the core developers disappear.

## For organizations: not an ideal, but risk management

For individuals, privacy is often seen as an abstract ideal. For organizations, each property is a practical need:

- **Privacy** is risk management and compliance with data protection laws.
- **Security** is protecting assets and reputation.
- **Running your own node** is "on-premise" deployment, which many banks and agencies still require.
- **Censorship resistance** means not depending on the goodwill of a competitor or a country. See [Neutral blockspace](../enterprise/neutral-blockspace.mdx).

## For ETH holders: stability

The biggest practical benefit that decentralization and CROPS bring is **stability**:

- The rules don't change at anyone's whim. There's no one to persuade, bribe or coerce.
- No one can freeze your assets at the protocol level. There is no "kill switch."
- The network doesn't depend on any single company or country. In 2022, when most blocks were built through relays that filtered out transactions under US sanctions, those transactions still made it onto the chain, just more slowly. See [Neutral infrastructure](../government/global-neutrality.mdx).
- It has run continuously since July 30, 2015, and has never stopped producing blocks.

For many kinds of applications, these things are only "nice to have." For a **store of value**, they are the main reason people entrust it with their wealth. This is where CROPS stops being an ideal and becomes a practical strength.

## The age of artificial intelligence

Vitalik believes that in an age when artificial intelligence is upending many industries, stability will be valued even more. As technology, jobs and markets change faster than ever, an asset whose rules don't change, that no one can take from you, and that can survive for many decades will be a rare thing to lean on.

## Summary

- CROPS: censorship resistance and capture resistance, open source, privacy, security. The Ethereum Foundation treats these as non-negotiable.
- For organizations, CROPS is risk management. For ETH holders, CROPS is stability.
- Stability is what a store of value needs most, and it will be even more precious in the age of artificial intelligence.

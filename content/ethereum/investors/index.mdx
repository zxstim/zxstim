This section is for people who hold ETH or want to learn about it. If you don't know what ETH is yet, read [What is ether (ETH)?](../basics/getting-started/what-is-ether.mdx) first.

The backbone of this section is an argument Vitalik Buterin made in November 2026: **ETH's value comes from its role as a store of value, and that role is a direct result of decentralization and CROPS** (censorship resistance and capture resistance, open source, privacy, security). Burned fees are still good, but they are not the foundation.
